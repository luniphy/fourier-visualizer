Fourier Series Visualizer

Welcome to this little visual demonstration on Fourier series.

Here you can adjust the parameters of a periodic function \(f(x)\) and the program will build a Fourier series out of it.
The Fourier series shows that any periodic function can be written as a sum of \(\sin(x)\) and \(\cos(x)\).

\( f(x) = \frac{a_0}{2} + \sum_{n = 1}^{\infty} \; ( a_n \; \cos(nx) + b_n \; \sin(nx) ) \)

\( a_0 = \frac{1}{\pi} \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \; f(x) \;\mathrm{d}x, \; \; a_n = \frac{1}{\pi} \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \; f(x) \; \cos(nx) \;\mathrm{d}x, \; \; b_n = \frac{1}{\pi} \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \; f(x) \; \sin(nx) \;\mathrm{d}x \)

Choose a periodic function type and adjust its amplitude \(A\), frequency \(f\), phase \(\phi\) and offset \(\mathrm{O}\).
