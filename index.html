Fourier Series Visualizer

Welcome to this demonstration on how a Fourier series work.

Here you create any periodic function and let the program create the Fourier series out of it.
With this you'll see that any periodic function can be written as a sum of \(\sin(x)\) and \(\cos(x)\).

Choose a periodic function type and adjust its amplitude \(A\), frequency \(f\) and offset \(\phi\).
